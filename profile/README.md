## Our mission

> Our mission is to build the systems and technologies that enable financial intelligence to transcend institutions and borders, understand how time, risk, and value shape markets, and extend to everyone the power to understand and shape our financial future.

## Lowering the cost of intelligent market participation

Financial markets run on a continuous chain:

```text
Data  ->  Research  ->  Decision  ->  Risk  ->  Execution  ->  Feedback
```

For most developers and organizations, that chain is fragmented across data vendors, notebooks, backtesting tools, broker APIs, execution services, and internal infrastructure.

**QuantDinger brings the chain into one open system.**

It gives developers, quantitative researchers, and AI agents a shared foundation for researching markets, building strategies, validating evidence, controlling risk, and operating across crypto, equities, futures, and forex.

## More than a trading tool

QuantDinger is not a black-box trading bot, a strategy marketplace, or another charting application. It is an extensible financial infrastructure layer designed to support the full lifecycle of systematic trading.

| Layer | Infrastructure |
| --- | --- |
| **Market connectivity** | Market data, fundamentals, news, exchange APIs, and broker integrations. |
| **Research and intelligence** | Indicators, signals, experiments, AI analysis, and multi-agent workflows. |
| **Strategy engineering** | Reproducible backtesting, validation, portfolio logic, and risk controls. |
| **Execution** | Paper and live trading, order lifecycle management, reconciliation, and alerts. |
| **Operations** | Positions, accounts, logs, metrics, strategy health, and human oversight. |
| **Developer platform** | APIs, MCP-compatible tools, self-hosted deployment, and custom extensions. |

## Built for agents. Governed by humans.

The next generation of financial software will be operated by both people and AI agents. QuantDinger is designed to become the infrastructure those agents can safely use.

- Agents can discover market context, generate research, and build strategy logic.
- Reproducible experiments separate ideas from evidence.
- Deterministic controls govern risk, permissions, and execution.
- Paper-first workflows keep operators in control before capital is deployed.
- Audit trails and observability make every critical action inspectable.

## Open infrastructure creates trust

Financial systems should not require blind trust. Open source makes the infrastructure inspectable, deployable, and adaptable to different markets, brokers, models, and operating requirements.

Our engineering principles are straightforward:

- **Open by default** — inspect the system, extend it, and operate it on your own infrastructure.
- **Reproducible by design** — keep research inputs, experiments, and results comparable.
- **Safe at the boundary** — separate AI assistance from deterministic execution controls.
- **Observable in production** — keep orders, positions, risk, logs, and system health visible.
- **Composable at every layer** — bring your own data, models, brokers, strategies, and agents.
