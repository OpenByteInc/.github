## Building the future of quantitative trading

Open Byte Inc. is the company behind **QuantDinger**, an open-source platform that brings market research, strategy development, backtesting, live execution, and AI-assisted workflows into one unified environment.

Our goal is simple: give researchers, developers, and traders the infrastructure they need to move from an idea to a tested, observable trading system.

## Our projects

| Project | What it does | Explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **QuantDinger** | Core AI-native quantitative trading platform for crypto, stocks, and forex | [Repository](https://github.com/OpenByteInc/QuantDinger) |
| **QuantDinger Vue** | Official web workspace for research, strategy, trading, and operations | [Repository](https://github.com/OpenByteInc/QuantDinger-Vue) |
| **QuantDinger Mobile** | Mobile and lightweight web client for the QuantDinger ecosystem | [Repository](https://github.com/OpenByteInc/QuantDinger-Mobile) |

## What you can build

- Research markets with AI-assisted and multi-agent workflows
- Design and backtest quantitative strategies against historical data
- Connect research to live multi-market execution
- Monitor strategies, positions, risk, and operational health
- Extend the platform with your own data sources, models, and integrations

## Start with QuantDinger

```bash
git clone https://github.com/OpenByteInc/QuantDinger.git

```
